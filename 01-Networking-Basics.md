###### 1.1 Network Types 



* No one individual or group owns the internet.



* The internet is a worldwide collection of interconnected networks.



* Interconnected networks (internetwork or internet for short).





###### 1.1.4 Local Networks



* Networks range from small simple networks to massive complex networks, depending on scenario and number of connected devices. 



* SOHO Networks are Small Office / Home Office networks.



* SOHO Networks allow users to share resources like printers, documents, pictures, and music between other local users.



* Large networks in businesses can be used to advertise and sell products, order supplies and communicate with customers.



* Communication via a network is usually more efficient and cost effective than traditional mail or long distance calls.



* The internet is considered a "network of networks".





###### 1.1.5 Mobile Devices



* Internet is made up of more than just Desktop and Laptop computers.



* Other networked devices include mobile phones, tablets, smartwatches and smart glasses.



* Smartphones can connect to the internet from almost anywhere, and has the functionality to take pictures, call people, play music and media and be tracked using its built in GPS receiver. It also has a touch screen computer built in.



* Tablets have similar functionality to smartphones, with the addition of a larger screen size, making them ideal for watching videos or reading books.



* Smartwatches can connect to smartphones and allow users to read notifications from their phones like messages without having to physically find them when on the go. 



* Smartwatches also have built in health monitoring features, such as heart rate monitoring and step counters.
